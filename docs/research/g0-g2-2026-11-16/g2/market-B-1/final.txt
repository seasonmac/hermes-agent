**Decision memo**

Return the proposal for revision. The supplied evidence does not substantiate the claims that paid users tripled or that the market grew 30%, and it does not support the proposed TAM estimate.

Correction C1 clarifies that P’s 1.2 million figure counts cumulative global registrations, including free and inactive accounts. Q’s 400,000 figure estimates monthly active accounts in the EU, with a reported uncertainty of ±15%. The estimate is unaudited and includes no paid-seat count. The figures use different populations and time windows, and neither provides a comparable paid baseline or a paid conversion rate.

R cites only P. That citation reports one vendor’s registrations and does not substantiate the 30% market-growth claim. Multiplying 1.2 million by $100 yields $120 million per month arithmetically. The proposal provides no basis for treating those registrations as addressable paying units or for applying that price to them.

Before reconsidering, obtain comparable paid counts across two periods under the same definition and geography, a market-growth source with a defined market and comparison period, and a TAM model grounded in eligible buyers, billable units per buyer, and supported pricing.
