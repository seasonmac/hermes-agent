**Decision memo**

Exclude the paid-user tripling claim and the proposed TAM estimate from underwriting. Give the 30% market-growth claim no weight until it has an independent source.

**Evidence**

- **P, October 2026** reports 1.2 million cumulative registered accounts worldwide, including free and inactive accounts.
- **Q, November 2026** estimates 400,000 monthly active accounts in the EU. C1 corrects the description of these accounts as paid seats. The estimate is sampled, carries stated uncertainty of ±15%, and is unaudited.
- **R** cites only P, which provides a product account count and no market-growth data.

Dividing Q by P cannot establish activity or paid conversion because the figures cover different geographies and time windows. Multiplying P’s account count by the proposal’s $100 monthly price yields $120 million per month on paper. A TAM estimate requires a count of potential buyers across a defined market and a supported price per billing unit.

**Evidence needed**

Obtain paid-user counts for two comparable periods using the same definition and geography. Verify paid conversion for the relevant cohort. For TAM, define the target segments and geographies, count eligible buyers or seats, and validate pricing against the chosen billing unit. For market growth, obtain a dated series for that same market.
