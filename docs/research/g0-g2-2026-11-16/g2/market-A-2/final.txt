**Decision memo**

Hold the proposal’s claims that paid users tripled, the market grew 30%, and TAM is $120 million per month. The supplied evidence supports none of these estimates.

P’s October press release reports 1.2 million cumulative registered accounts worldwide, including free and inactive accounts. Under correction C1, Q’s November note estimates 400,000 monthly active accounts in the EU, with a stated ±15% margin. The estimate is unaudited and gives no paid-seat count. The figures differ in geography and account definition, and the sources provide no common baseline period or paid conversion rate. R cites only P, which supplies no market growth series.

Before revisiting the proposal, obtain comparable paid-user counts for two defined periods in the same geography, a market growth series with a defined scope and period, and an independently supported count of eligible buyers or seats with a price tied to that unit. Multiplying cumulative registrations by an assumed $100 monthly price does not establish TAM.

**Post-correction self-review.** I corrected my initial reading of Q and checked each proposal claim against the revised definitions, geography, and available time periods. The evidence establishes neither a paid-user trend nor a defensible TAM.
