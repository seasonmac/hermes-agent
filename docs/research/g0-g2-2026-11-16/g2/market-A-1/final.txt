**Decision memo**

Remove the claims that paid users tripled, that the market grew 30%, and that TAM can be estimated by multiplying 1.2 million by $100 per month. Keep the market opportunity unquantified pending better evidence.

P reports 1.2 million cumulative registered accounts globally, including free and inactive accounts. Q estimates 400,000 monthly active accounts in the EU from a sample, with reported uncertainty of ±15%. The estimate is unaudited and paid status is unknown. The two figures differ in geography and definition, and there is no common baseline or paid conversion rate. R cites only P, which provides no market-wide growth data.

To assess paid growth, obtain comparable paid-account or paid-seat counts for two periods in the same geography. To estimate TAM, define the target geography, count eligible buyers or seats across the market, and validate annual pricing for that same unit. The proposed $100 monthly price is an assumption.
